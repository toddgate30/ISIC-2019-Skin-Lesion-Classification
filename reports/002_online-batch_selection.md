# Online Batch Selection
## Goal:
I'm curious if Online Batch Selection methods can improve the training process. I'm especially interested in diversified batch selection (DivBS). I'm wondering if it will help the data to refrain from overfitting (or at least give a small increase in validation accuracy). I am also curious how it handles under class imbalance since it seeks to train with a diversified selection of points every time.

## Test:
I'm currently testing Uniform sampling vs. DivBS with and without weighted Cross Entropy Loss. I will record my observations when they finish.
